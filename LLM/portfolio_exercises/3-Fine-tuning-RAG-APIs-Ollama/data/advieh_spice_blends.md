# Authentic Persian Advieh Spice Blends

Advieh is the cornerstone spice mixture of Persian cuisine. Unlike garam masala or curry powder, Persian advieh relies heavily on dried rose petals, green cardamom, and cinnamon, giving stews and rice dishes a distinctive floral, warm, and sweet-savory fragrance.

## 1. Advieh-e Polo (Rice Spice Blend)
Used exclusively in layered rice dishes (such as Lubia Polo, Morasa Polo, and Shirin Polo). It is sprinkled between the rice layers before steaming.

* **Culinary Ratio:**
  - 3 tbsp Ground Dried Rose Petals (Gol-e Mohammadi)
  - 2 tbsp Ground Cinnamon (Darchin)
  - 1 tbsp Ground Green Cardamom (Hel)
  - 1 tbsp Ground Cumin (Zireh)
  - 1/2 tsp Ground Nutmeg (Joz-e Hendi)
* **Secret Rule:** Never add turmeric to Advieh-e Polo; turmeric will discolor the white rice layers and mask the saffron aroma.

## 2. Advieh-e Khoresh (Stew Spice Blend)
Used for slow-simmered stews like Ghormeh Sabzi, Gheimeh, and Fesenjan.

* **Culinary Ratio:**
  - 2 tbsp Ground Dried Black Pepper
  - 2 tbsp Ground Cinnamon
  - 2 tbsp Ground Turmeric (Zardchubeh)
  - 1 tbsp Ground Cardamom
  - 1 tbsp Ground Coriander Seeds
  - 1 tsp Ground Ginger
  - 1/2 tsp Ground Cloves (Mikhang)
* **Secret Rule:** Advieh-e Khoresh must be bloomed in hot oil with onions for 45 to 60 seconds before adding meat or liquids to activate the fat-soluble flavor compounds.

## 3. Advieh-e Torshi (Pickle Spice Blend)
Used specifically for Persian pickled vegetables (Torshi Liteh, Torshi Sir).

* **Culinary Ratio:**
  - 2 tbsp Ground Nigella Seeds (Siah Daneh)
  - 2 tbsp Ground Angelica (Golpar)
  - 1 tbsp Ground Cumin
  - 1 tbsp Ground Coriander
  - 1 tbsp Ground Fennel Seeds
* **Secret Rule:** Golpar is essential here to prevent digestive gas and impart the pungent herbal aroma unique to authentic Persian pickles.
